Meeting requests create durable invitation emails for the registered account addresses. The browser immediately dispatches their server-generated notification IDs; the background worker also recovers pending/failed meeting emails if the browser disconnects. Messages include the SAST start, duration and an absolute link to the private meeting. Accept/decline and cancellation/end notifications use the same outbox.

The host and invitees who have not declined receive separate reminders at 60 and 30 minutes before the start. Only thresholds after the meeting was created are eligible. The worker runs once per minute, so delivery attempts normally begin within a minute of each threshold; provider/inbox delays are outside the app's control. If the worker misses the one-hour window until the thirty-minute threshold, only the thirty-minute reminder is sent. Reminders expire at the start. Invitations expire at the end. Cancellation, ending, declined invitations and inactive accounts suppress pending invitations/reminders at claim time.

Stable event keys, database leases and provider idempotency protect retries. Failed sends retain bounded automatic retries; provider acceptance is distinct from confirmed delivery. The meeting worker only processes meeting notifications and does not activate the unrelated operational jobs.

That script installs pg_cron/pg_net, restricts transport tables and registers `smartbridge-meeting-emails`. Each invocation receives a random single-use, five-minute credential; service-role-only validation consumes it. No browser credential or long-lived cron secret is introduced. Pause with `cron.unschedule('smartbridge-meeting-emails')`; reinstall the script to resume.
