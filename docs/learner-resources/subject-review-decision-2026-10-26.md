# SmartBridge FET — Subject advisory review decision
Review date: 26 October 2026
Scope: Geography pollution dome and Life Sciences human heart lesson drafts and combined branded visual poster.
Reviewer role: AI-assisted editorial and curriculum quality assessment; **not** a formally appointed DBE subject advisor or independently accredited human moderation.

## Decision: conditional content approval only — NOT cleared for publication

### Geography | Grade 12 | Urban climates / pollution domes
**Curriculum placement: PASS.** DBE Grade 12 Geography exam guidelines explicitly address definition, causes, effects and mitigation of pollution domes. 
**Written scientific explanation: PASS WITH REVISIONS.** The original prose distinguishes air temperature inversion from pollutants and avoids treating the inversion as an impermeable cap.
**Assessment completeness: REVISE.** Add explicit, markable questions on (a) three pollution-reduction strategies; (b) economic, social and environmental impacts; (c) distinguishing an urban heat island from a pollution dome; (d) interpreting day/night city-climate sketches.
**Original poster: REVISE.** Diagram claims that an inversion *prevents* all vertical air movement and the visual's lid analogy are too absolute. Replace with “reduces vertical mixing/dispersion”. Recheck the illustrated vertical temperature/airflow annotations to avoid contradictory rising arrows suggesting easy passage through the inversion. Avoid treating inversions as the sole cause of pollution domes. Separate city heat-island physics from pollution accumulation. Check fine-print legibility on phones.

### Life Sciences | **Grade 10**, Transport Systems in Mammals (Human)
**Curriculum placement: CRITICAL CORRECTION.** The heart, circulation and associated vessels are Grade 10 content in official Life Sciences CAPS and WCED ATP. Previous “Grades 11–12” label is incorrect and MUST be replaced everywhere, including final artwork and library metadata.
**Written scientific explanation: PASS WITH REVISIONS.** The named four chambers, venae cavae, pulmonary vessels, AV valves, aortic/pulmonary valves and flow sequence are broadly accurate.
**Assessment completeness: REVISE.** Add simple heart-cycle items (atrial systole, ventricular systole, diastole), structure/function of arteries, veins and capillaries, and analysis of an unlabeled heart diagram to align with Grade 10 transport-systems content. Avoid claiming this single lesson covers the whole topic.
**Original poster: REVISE.** Grade shown as 11 & 12 is wrong. Check that *every* blood-flow arrow points along the real path (some stylised arrows are hard to follow); add labels for pulmonary and aortic semilunar valves, septum and pericardium as appropriate. Verify anatomical right/left legend and point-to-structure lines against a trusted anatomy chart. Do not interpret color alone as oxygenation.

### Shared release requirements
- Keep professional SmartBridge FET header and footer; add exact Grade, CAPS topic, accessible text alternative, publication edition/date and reporting contact.
- Redraw final charts as original, scalable vectors and test at a narrow phone width. Treat the combined existing poster as a *reference draft*, not a student-ready verified graphic.
- Create revised assessment items with memorandum and mark allocation; independent qualified subject educator must check content/labels.
- Verify actual learner sign-in, access permissions, resource card, opening/downloading, search filters and mobile behavior before changing status to published.
- Distinguish free and premium access based on the agreed product entitlement scheme; do not publish paid resources inadvertently as free.

**Review outcome:** Grade 12 Geography and Grade 10 Life Sciences lesson text may progress to final editing. **Diagram approval withheld**. **Overall publication approval withheld** until corrections, educator moderation and live-learner checks are documented.
