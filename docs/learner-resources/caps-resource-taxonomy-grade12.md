## Life Sciences > Grade 12 > Term 1 > Human Reproduction
Official sequence and separate learning resources:
1. **Male reproductive system:** one diagram for anatomy (testis, epididymis, sperm duct/vas deferens, seminal vesicle, prostate, urethra, penis, scrotum) and one set of structure/function notes.
2. **Female reproductive system:** separate anatomy diagram (ovary, fallopian tube, uterus, endometrium, cervix, vagina) and matching notes. NEVER combine with male anatomy on the same lesson poster.
3. **Puberty:** changes and hormone regulation; separate explanatory resource.
4. **Gametogenesis:** sperm production and egg-cell formation; differentiated process diagrams; link to meiosis without detailed individual stages.
5. **Menstrual cycle:** ovarian and uterine cycles, oestrogen/progesterone, FSH and LH, clear readable charts.
6. **Fertilisation and zygote-to-blastocyst development:** sequence panel.
7. **Implantation and gestation:** implantation, placenta and pregnancy; appropriate separate process diagram.

CAPS outlines human reproduction over three weeks / 12 teaching hours in Term 1. Diagram captions and metadata must match these topic boundaries; no overloaded collage.

## Geography > Grade 12 > Climate and weather > Urban climates > Pollution domes
Entirely independent Geography resource, not indexed or bundled with Life Sciences. Do not invent a term on the poster; grade+subject+topic suffice. Pollution domes: definition, causes, effects, reduction strategies. Repair existing text that says an inversion **stops** air movement: use **reduces vertical mixing/dispersion**. Avoid claiming an inversion is the only cause.
