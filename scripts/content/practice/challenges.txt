zuhl/10/4|Ukuqhathanisa inhliziyo nesigubhu kunamuphi umthelela emoyeni wombhalo?|Kwenza imizwa izwakale inamandla, kodwa akukuvezi okuqukethwe incwadi.|Kufakazela ukuthi kukhona isigubhu sangempela egumbini.|Kusitshela amamaki akhe esikole.|Kufakazela ukuthi incwadi isivuliwe.|Isifaniso sigcizelela ukushaya kwenhliziyo namandla omuzwa. Umthelela wolimi uyabonakala, kodwa okuqukethwe incwadi akukashiwo embhalweni.
zuhl/11/2|Ukwandisa amahora kuphela kuzoyixazulula yini inkinga echazwe lapha?|Akukafakazelwa; ezokuthutha zabafundi nazo zidinga ukucatshangelwa.|Yebo; isikhathi setekisi asihlobene nokufinyelela.|Cha; akekho umfundi oweseka indawo yokufunda.|Yebo; ikomiti seliqinisekise amatekisi asebusuku.|Umbhalo uhlanganisa ukweseka indawo yokufunda nomkhawulo wezokuthutha. Isixazululo kufanele sibhekane nalowo mkhawulo kunokucabanga ukuthi amahora kuphela anele.
zuhl/11/3|Ukushintsha isimangalo sibe abanye abafundi bangazuza kuyilungisa kanjani inkinga?|Kunciphisa ukweqisa, kodwa kusadingeka ucwaningo olubanzi lokuhlola inzuzo.|Kufakazela ukuthi bonke abafundi bazophumelela.|Kwenza ukuqhathanisa kungadingeki.|Kufakazela ukuthi akekho ongazuza.|Ulimi olucophelelayo aludali ubufakazi obusha. Umbiko womfundi oyedwa usenomkhawulo, ngakho isimangalo esilinganiselayo sisadinga ukuhlolwa okufanele.
zuhl/11/4|Kungani umehluko phakathi kokuthi umbalisi uzohamba nokulinda kwakhe usebenza kahle?|Uveza ukungqubuzana kwesinqumo nesenzo ngaphandle kokuchaza yonke imbangela.|Ufakazela ukuthi umbalisi akakaze afune ukuhamba.|Usitshela igama lomuntu olindelwe.|Ubonisa ukuthi okhiye bayakhuluma.|Ukuma nokulinda ngemva kwesinqumo sokuhamba kwakha ukungaqiniseki. Lo mehluko uvumela ukuhumusha okusekelwayo ngaphandle kokwengeza indaba engashiwo embhalweni.
zuhl/12/2|Ukususa igama kuphela kwenza umbiko ube ubufakazi obuphelele bembangela yini?|Cha; kunciphisa amandla esimangalo, kodwa imithelela yezinguquko ayikahlukaniswa.|Yebo; igama elilodwa lithatha indawo yocwaningo.|Cha; ukwanda ngo-20% akusho lutho nhlobo.|Yebo; izincwadi neklabhu azikwazi ukuba nomthelela.|Ukulungisa ulimi kuthuthukisa indlela isimangalo esibekwe ngayo. Izinto eziningi zishintshe ndawonye, ngakho ucwaningo lusadingeka ukuhlukanisa izimbangela.
zuhl/12/3|Ukushintsha abangamavila kube abangekho kulungisa wonke umbiko yini?|Cha; umongo wesithombe namazwi abafundi abanezinkinga zokuthutha kusadingeka.|Yebo; igama elilodwa liqeda zonke izinkinga zobufakazi.|Cha; isikhathi sesithombe asibalulekile.|Yebo; izihloko azidingi bufakazi.|Ukususa ukwahlulela okubi kuyasiza, kodwa akulungisi isithombe esidukisayo nezwi elibalulekile elishiyiwe. Umbiko olinganiselayo udinga umongo nobufakazi obanele.
zuhl/12/4|Ukuxhamazela kwesitimela kunamuphi umthelela uma kuqhathaniswa nokuhlehla kukaNoma?|Kwandisa ukungezwani phakathi kokuya phambili nokungaqiniseki kwakhe.|Kufakazela konke okubhalwe encwadini.|Kubonisa ukuthi isitimela singumuntu wangempela.|Kufakazela ukuthi ngeke ahambe nanini.|Ukwenza samuntu kwakha umuzwa wokuphuthuma. Isenzo sikaNoma esiphikisayo sakha ukungezwani, kodwa asisiniki isinqumo sakhe sokugcina.
