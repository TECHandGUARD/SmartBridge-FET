@math/10/2|Geometry and functions
A triangle has angles 48° and 62°. Find its third angle.|70°|110°|80°|132°|Interior angles total 180°: 180 − 48 − 62 = 70°. State the angle-sum reason in a written solution.
Find the midpoint of A(2; 4) and B(8; 10).|(5; 7)|(6; 6)|(10; 14)|(3; 3)|Average the x-coordinates and y-coordinates separately: ((2+8)/2; (4+10)/2).
For f(x) = 2x + 3, what is f(−2)?|−1|7|1|−7|Substitute with brackets: 2(−2)+3 = −4+3 = −1. A negative input does not always give a negative output.
Which point lies on y = x²?|(−3; 9)|(−3; −9)|(2; 8)|(0; 1)|Squaring means multiplying a number by itself: (−3)² = 9. The minus sign is inside the brackets.
