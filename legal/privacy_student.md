## 2. Information and purposes
Depending on the features you use, the platform receives:
- Account and contact information, login identifiers, account role and security records to register you, authenticate access, prevent misuse and contact you about your account.
- Grade, subjects, learning activity, quiz answers, study plans, attendance and progress to provide educational tools and progress reports.
- Tutor applications, qualifications and verification records to assess applications and manage suitable access.
- Booking details, messages, cancellation reasons, payment references and transaction status to organise lessons, address disputes and keep transaction records. Never send passwords, PINs or full card details to support.
- Guardian relationships, consent records and limited supporting evidence to check authority and decide what information may be shared.
- Content you submit, support requests, incident reports and relevant technical logs to provide features, protect users and investigate problems.

Required information is identified in the relevant form. Without essential account, booking or lawful guardian-authority information, we may be unable to provide that feature. Optional media or marketing permission must not be made a condition of unrelated learning access.

## 3. Why processing is permitted
We assess the appropriate legal basis for each purpose, including providing an agreed service, meeting legal duties, protecting legitimate interests where lawful, and specific consent where needed. For a child's information, a separate lawful authorisation under POPIA's child-information rules is required; ordinary adult contractual or legitimate-interest grounds alone do not bypass those rules. Our standard participation process requires prior informed consent from a competent person whose authority can be verified.

## 4. Who can receive information
Authorised staff and service providers receive information needed for their tasks. Assigned tutors receive the lesson information necessary to teach; linked parents receive permitted learner progress information. Neither group has unrestricted access to a learner's private account. A disputed relationship must be reviewed before disclosure. Providers include Supabase for account/database/storage services and AWS Amplify for website hosting; email and enabled AI services may receive the information necessary for those functions. Payment processing follows the provider identified at checkout. This notice does not mean payments are currently enabled.

Content posted in shared study spaces is visible to their permitted participants. Public tutor profiles and introduction media can be seen publicly. Private credentials, completed agreements, learner identity documents and bank details must never be posted there. Information may also be disclosed where lawfully required by a court, regulator or protective reporting obligation. We do not authorise selling children's personal information or using it for unrelated advertising.

## 5. International processing and AI
SmartBridge FET's database project is configured in Supabase's eu-west-1 region. Hosting, delivery, support and enabled AI processing can involve other countries; the database region does not establish that every copy stays there. Transfers require an appropriate lawful basis and safeguards under POPIA. Contact us for applicable provider details. AI features use the configured OpenAI API when available. Do not enter names, identity numbers, contact details, confidential forms, health information or payment information in prompts. Provider logging and retention can still apply; we do not promise zero retention. The Service, Media and AI Privacy Addendum gives more detail.

## 6. Retention and security
We keep information only while necessary for its recorded purpose or another lawful retention basis, such as transaction evidence, tax duties, dispute resolution or security. Closure of an account does not automatically require destruction of records the law requires us to retain. Retained information must be limited to that purpose, access restricted and erased or de-identified when no longer justified. Backup copies may expire through provider backup cycles rather than disappear instantly. Ask support for the applicable category and retention basis; a subscription is not permission for indefinite retention.

Access permissions, private document storage and versioned consent records support privacy. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Report suspected exposure promptly without reposting leaked information. We will investigate, contain incidents and make the notifications required by law; no contractual clause removes those duties.

## 7. Your choices and rights
Contact support to request access, correction, deletion where applicable, an explanation of processing, withdrawal of consent or an objection permitted by law. We may require proportionate identity and guardian-authority checks, using a private route. We will explain any lawful refusal, applicable fee or retained category and available challenge route. Withdrawal does not undo lawful earlier processing and may prevent a feature that depends on that consent. Request human review of significant automated decisions affecting you.

You can complain directly to the [Information Regulator](https://inforegulator.org.za/) without first completing our internal process. This notice does not restrict statutory complaint deadlines or access rights.

## 8. Browser storage and communications
The web app uses browser storage for authentication and preferences and can retain tutor-profile drafts on a device. Sign out on shared devices and avoid saving confidential information there. Browser controls can clear storage but may sign you out or remove drafts. Marketing permissions are separate from necessary account, safety and booking communications. You can object to marketing through support or an available unsubscribe route. This notice does not authorise non-essential tracking or marketing that requires separate consent.

## 9. Changes
Material changes will be explained before taking effect where required. A new purpose requiring consent needs a new choice; changing this notice cannot retrospectively authorise it. Published wording describes obligations and available processes, not an independent certification of POPIA compliance.
