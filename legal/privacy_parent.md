## Parent information and authority
We use your account/contact information, your stated relationship to a learner, consent decisions and verification records to manage authorised participation and progress access. We may receive relationship details from the learner or the person requesting a link. We must check contested or unclear authority before disclosing learner information. Being a parent, knowing a learner's email or possessing a completed form does not automatically unlock the learner's account.

## Information you may see
A permitted parent link provides the progress/report information authorised for that relationship. It does not give access to passwords, every private conversation, unrelated learners or tutor credentials. Access for an adult learner requires their permission or another lawful basis. A child's views, safety and applicable legal rights must be considered when handling a guardian request.

## Specific consent
Core participation, public photographs/testimonials, recording, direct marketing and optional AI processing are different purposes. Core guardian consent does not authorise all of them. Optional permissions must identify the purpose, information, recipients and withdrawal route and must remain unselected unless expressly chosen. If no optional permission is recorded, treat it as refused. Do not send another person's data without authority.

## Withdrawal and disputes
Ask support to change consent, correct a relationship, challenge access or request deletion. We will check authority using a proportionate private process and explain the effect on services and any lawful retention. Where guardians disagree or a safety concern exists, access may be restricted while the facts are reviewed. Do not put family-court orders, identity documents or completed consent forms into shared study rooms.

## Records and complaints
Store a copy of the version and choices you sign. Completed forms must be restricted to authorised staff and the relevant account owner, with necessary lawful disclosure only. For the general retention, providers, international transfers, security and regulator complaint route, see the main Privacy Notice. Reading this addendum is not proof that guardian verification has occurred.
