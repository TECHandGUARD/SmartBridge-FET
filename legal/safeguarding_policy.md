# Child Safeguarding, Conduct and Complaints Policy

SmartBridge FET | TECH AND GUARD (Pty) Ltd
Version 2.0 | Effective 17 October 2026

## 1. Purpose and contact
Learner safety applies to administrators, tutors, parents and users. Report concerns privately to [aneleq@techandguard.co.za](mailto:aneleq@techandguard.co.za), with "Safeguarding" in the subject. This is not an emergency response service and continuous monitoring is not promised. If a person is in immediate danger, seek urgent help from the relevant emergency services or police and a trusted adult; do not wait for an app response.

## 2. Preventive duties
The company must check tutor identity, declared qualifications/status and suitability for the proposed activities, including any legally required register/background checks, before allowing the relevant child-facing work. An application approval or profile badge must not imply checks that were never performed. Student tutors must not be presented as qualified teachers unless they are. Guardian authority must be established before relying on guardian consent; an unverified email link is insufficient.

Use agreed teaching channels, appropriate material and age-appropriate supervision. Any in-person arrangement needs separate safety and location arrangements with the competent person; a generic online booking is not approval of an unsupervised home visit. Learners may leave a session that feels unsafe and report the reason.

## 3. Prohibited conduct
Sexual or romantic approaches to learners, grooming, intimate-image requests, threats, discrimination, humiliation, corporal punishment, coercion, exploitation, secret meetings and demands to conceal contact are prohibited. No one may buy a child's silence, pressure a learner to withdraw a report or retaliate for a good-faith complaint. General consent never authorises abuse. Tutors must not use learner contact details for private marketing or share confidential records with unrelated people.

## 4. Media, recordings and information
Do not record sessions or publish learner names, faces, voices, marks or testimonials without an appropriate lawful basis and specific permissions. Core participation consent does not provide those permissions. Never post ID documents, completed forms, payment details or allegations in public study areas. Use proportionate private evidence collection; do not circulate suspected illegal images or ask a child to resend them.

## 5. Receiving a report
Provide the account/session reference, a factual description, approximate time and the safest way to contact you. Share only necessary evidence through a private route arranged with support. Listen calmly, avoid leading questions, do not blame the learner and do not promise absolute secrecy. Explain that information may need to reach authorised protective services. Staff and tutors must meet any applicable mandatory reporting duties without waiting for an internal investigation.

## 6. Protective response and fair review
On receipt, an authorised administrator must assess immediate risk, restrict relevant access/contact where necessary, preserve limited evidence securely and refer to appropriate authorities where required. Protective suspension is precautionary and is not a finding of guilt. Separate affected users where practicable and explain safe next steps. Handle reports involving an administrator with another authorised reviewer wherever possible.

The respondent should receive enough information and an opportunity to respond where safe and lawful. Protect a child's identity and avoid alerting a person where doing so could increase risk or compromise a lawful investigation. Document reasons, decisions, referrals and review dates. Restrict records to people who need them; no guarantee of total confidentiality is made when disclosure is necessary for protection or law.

## 7. Complaints, outcomes and appeal
Possible outcomes include correction, warning, further checks, access limits, removal, service/refund remedies or referral to a competent authority. Communicate appropriate outcomes without exposing another person's confidential information. Request a human review through support, identifying the decision and reasons challenged. No internal appeal requirement prevents external reporting or legal remedies.

We aim to acknowledge ordinary complaints within two business days and provide a response or progress update within ten business days. Safety reports require priority risk assessment; these targets are not permission to delay an urgent protective action or statutory report. Publication of this policy does not mean screening, training or every operational safeguard has already been completed; those duties require evidence and ongoing work.
