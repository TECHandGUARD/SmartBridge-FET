## Purpose
Record the learner's participation and the competent person's authority where required. A staff member must verify the request before enabling any account/report access. Do not collect national identity numbers, health details or banking information on this form.

## Choices
Permission for learning-platform participation does not automatically permit public learner photographs, session recordings, direct marketing, sensitive-data AI processing or disclosure to unrelated tutors/parents. Separate, specific consent is needed where applicable. Learner-approved report links remain subject to lawful guardian-authority review.

## Declaration
I have reviewed the service and parent/student terms and understand the fee/cancellation policy. I will contact support if authority, consent or the learner's circumstances change. I understand withdrawal or a deletion request may require identity/authority verification and lawful record-retention review. Complete and retain this form. Submit only through a private channel arranged with support. Consent takes effect only after the competent person's authority has been checked.

## Information covered by core participation consent
Core participation involves the learner's account/contact details, grade/subjects, learning activity, bookings, authorised lesson communications, progress, consent and safety records. These support learning, account administration, appropriate parent reporting and protection. The main Privacy Notice explains necessary recipients/providers, overseas processing, retention and your rights. Only necessary information may be collected. Ask support about anything unclear before signing.

## Separate choices — complete each independently
Blank, ambiguous or unselected optional choices mean no permission. Do not bundle optional permission into core participation. This form does not grant any permission for public learner media, recording, marketing or identifying/sensitive AI processing; those require their own purpose-specific process.

## Withdrawal and verification
You can contact support to withdraw a consent, change a choice, challenge a relationship or exercise applicable privacy rights. Withdrawal may prevent the dependent feature; it does not reverse lawful earlier processing or erase records that must lawfully be retained. A request to link a parent account is not approved solely because this form was signed. Staff must verify authority and record the purpose/version/choices before relying on consent. If authority cannot be established, do not enable child-data processing based on this form.
