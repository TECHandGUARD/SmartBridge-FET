## Application and account records
We use your account details, stated university status, qualifications, subject competence, relevant SACE information, availability, rates and application records to assess and administer tutoring access. Verification may require proportionate supporting evidence supplied privately and checks with the relevant institution or issuing body where lawful. No applicant is guaranteed approval, bookings or income. Do not submit a criminal-history or health record unless a specific lawful and secure process has been explained.

## Public profile versus private documents
Your published tutor name, professional biography, subjects, displayed prices, approved photograph and introduction video are intended for the tutor directory and may be viewable without a login. Only upload media you are authorised to publish. Qualifications evidence, identity documents, full professional registration records, banking information and completed agreements belong in restricted records, never public introduction media. Ask support to correct or remove a profile; public copies made outside the platform may not disappear immediately.

## Session, finance and support data
Booking times, attendance, learner feedback, cancellations, messages and relevant transaction references help deliver lessons, resolve complaints and calculate any agreed earnings. A private financial schedule must specify commercial terms before paid tutoring begins. We do not invent a payout date or deduction from this notice. Financial details must be supplied only through an identified private process when required.

## Your duty to learners
Use learner information only for the authorised tutoring service and required protective reporting. Do not export contact lists, market to learners, share learner data with other tutors, post learner images, record sessions or send identifying learner information to AI tools without a separate lawful basis and any required permission. Keep only necessary records, secure your devices and tell support promptly about loss or unauthorised disclosure. Confidentiality survives the end of your tutoring account.

## Rights and records
You may request access, corrections, lawful deletion or a review of a disputed application decision through support. Necessary transaction, safety, complaint and contractual evidence may need restricted retention after account closure. The main Privacy Notice sets out provider processing, international transfers, security and complaints to the Information Regulator. This addendum does not appoint you as an employee or determine your legal employment status.
